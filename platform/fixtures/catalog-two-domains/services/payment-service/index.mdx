## Behaviour

Consumes `OrderPlaced.v1` through the payments domain's generated subscriber on central (`payments-sub.OrderPlaced.v1`: filter, retries, DLQ, delivered to this service's queue), decrypts `customerEmail` as a listed decryptor, captures funds and publishes `PaymentCaptured.v1` onto `payments-bus`. Looks orders up with the generated client for `GetOrder`.
