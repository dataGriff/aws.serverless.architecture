<ChannelInformation />

No service publishes here directly. Each domain bus routes its public events here through its generated public-forward rule (see `routes` on `orders-bus` and `payments-bus`), and one generated fan-out rule per domain routes everything except that domain's own events back out to that domain's bus (`x-fan-out-to`). The fan-out hop is deliberately not declared as `routes` here: with two or more domain buses routing in and `central-bus` routing back out, EventCatalog 4.12.3's build overflows the stack while rendering the event pages. The generated `rules/{domain}-fan-out.json` is the authoritative description of that hop.
