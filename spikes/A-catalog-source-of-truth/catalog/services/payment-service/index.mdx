## Behaviour

Consumes `OrderPlaced.v1` from `payments-bus` (delivered by the fan-out rule), decrypts `customerEmail` as a listed decryptor, captures funds and publishes `PaymentCaptured.v1`. Looks orders up with the generated client for `GetOrder`.
