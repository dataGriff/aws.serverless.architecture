## Behaviour

Commands arrive over the orders API (`POST /v1/orders`) and queries over `GET /v1/orders/{id}`; both are modelled as messages this service receives. Facts leave as events through the transactional outbox and the relay module onto `orders-bus`, and nowhere else: the generated public-forward rule on `orders-bus` routes public events to `central-bus`, and the fan-out rule on `central-bus` routes them on to every other domain bus (see the channels' `routes`). Everything this service consumes arrives on `orders-bus`, never from another domain's bus.
