## Behaviour

Commands arrive over the orders API (`POST /v1/orders`) and queries over `GET /v1/orders/{id}`; both are modelled as messages this service receives. Facts leave as events through the transactional outbox and the relay module onto `orders-bus`, and nowhere else. Each event has its own logical channel on that bus (`orders-bus.<detail-type>`, generated); the public ones route to `central-bus.<detail-type>` through the generated forward rule, the one bus-to-bus hop AWS allows. `PaymentCaptured.v1` reaches this service through the orders domain's generated subscriber on central (`orders-sub.PaymentCaptured.v1`), never through another domain's bus.
